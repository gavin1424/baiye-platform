# 香雲冷香館・天地五行靈石 / Film identity

## Style Prompt
An intimate Eastern collection gallery: original cinematic mountain silhouettes, slow drifting mist, quiet mineral close-ups and restrained gilded orbit lines. Images remain the subject. Warm Song-style Chinese titles, highly readable subtitles, and deliberate dissolves convey an international collector's salon rather than a fantasy game trailer. Concept images and simulated 3D remain explicitly identified, family oral history is never presented as verified footage.

## Colors
- #101D18 deep ink green: stage and shadows
- #C4A477 champagne gold: fine rules, orbit geometry and emphasis
- #F5F0E8 warm ivory: titles and captions
- #382B23 walnut: warm frame and landscape layers
- #819684 muted jade: distant mountain linework

## Typography
Noto Serif TC 400 for titles; Noto Sans TC 400 for readable captions. Official Google Fonts, SIL OFL 1.1. Portrait title 72–90px, caption 40–44px, provenance 27–30px at 1080px canvas. Captions remain at least 220px from the bottom.

## Motion
Slow image push-ins, 0.6s cross-dissolves, gentle finite orbit rotation, sparse seeded particles. No rapid flashes. Actual website footage is recorded from the deployed preview, then presented in a framed phone window.

## What NOT to Do
No invented composition, assay, weight, certificate, verified inventory or supernatural claims. No private location. No unrelated characters, film clips or music. No simulated checkout or fake QR. No unreadable miniature captions. No zoom-cropping that substitutes one product for another.
